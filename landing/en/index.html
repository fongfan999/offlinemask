OfflineMask Tiếng Việt

In English

AI doesn't need to know who you're talking about.

OfflineMask is a free desktop app that replaces names, phone numbers, account numbers and ID numbers with codes, on your computer, before you ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. When the AI answers, it puts the real details back.

Made for Vietnamese. The app's screens are in Vietnamese, and it is tuned for Vietnamese documents: Vietnamese names and addresses, phone numbers, citizen ID (CCCD) and tax codes. If your work is in Vietnamese, or you handle Vietnamese customers' data, it is for you. Other languages are planned.

1How it works

  1. Mask. Paste text or open a file. OfflineMask finds the private details and shows you what it will hide. You review, then mask: "Nguyễn Minh Anh, 0901 234 567" becomes "[TEN_1_5102], [SDT_1_5102]".
  2. Ask the AI as usual. Paste the masked copy into the AI you already use. It still understands the task, so it can summarise, draft a reply or analyse a table, without ever seeing who it is about.
  3. Get the full version back. Paste the AI's answer into OfflineMask. Every code turns back into the real detail, on your computer. A code the AI changed is left as it is for you to check; the app never guesses.

On macOS and Windows, Ẩn nhanh (quick mask) does the same inside any app: select text, for example in the ChatGPT message box, and press the OfflineMask button or ⌘⇧M / Ctrl+Shift+M to mask or restore it in place.

2What it finds

Built in: email addresses, phone numbers, and labelled bank account numbers, citizen ID (CCCD), passport numbers and tax codes. With the optional Vietnamese pack, a one-time download that takes about 1.2 GB on disk: people's names, addresses and organisations, card numbers and passwords.

In text that is not Vietnamese it still finds email addresses, phone numbers written with a country code (+1, +44...), and account, passport and tax numbers next to a label such as "account number" or "tax ID". It is not built to find names or addresses in English, and it does not recognise IBANs or other countries' ID numbers.

It finds most private details, not all of them, so always read the masked copy before you send it. In pasted text, select anything it missed to mask that too.

3Files it opens

Text, plus spreadsheets and documents: XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, DOCX, ODT, PDF, PNG, JPG. Spreadsheets and Word files keep their formatting; PDFs and pictures give a masked text copy. Scanned PDFs and pictures are read on your computer, with nothing downloaded or sent. You can also mask a whole folder at once, very large CSV files included.

4Private by design

  • Masking and restoring run entirely on your computer and work with no internet. The table that maps codes to real details never leaves it.
  • OfflineMask does not connect to any AI. You paste the masked copy yourself, into whichever AI you choose.
  • It goes online only to download the app and, if you want it, the Vietnamese pack. No account, no ads, no tracking. Check it yourself: turn off Wi-Fi and try it.
  • What you mask is forgotten when you quit, unless you set a passcode: history is then kept encrypted on your computer and deletes itself after the period you choose.

The full privacy policy is in Vietnamese: Quyền riêng tư. The source code is on GitHub.

5Download

Free, with no paid tier. The latest version is __OM_VERSION__.

  • Mac: Apple Silicon or Intel. The first time you open it, macOS asks about an app downloaded from the internet: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, once.
  • Windows 10 and later: Microsoft Store, or the installer.
  • Linux: .deb (Ubuntu, Debian) or .rpm (Fedora).

Any ordinary office computer will do: 64-bit, 4 GB of memory or more (8 GB recommended). The app itself is 10-20 MB.
