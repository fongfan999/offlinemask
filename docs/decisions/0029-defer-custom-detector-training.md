# 0029 — Defer custom fine-tuning; reassess a sustainable pretrained baseline

Date: 18 September 2026. Status: product direction revised following the owner's concern about sufficient data and long-term maintenance. No replacement checkpoint selected.

The owner questioned whether maintaining a custom MiniLM privacy detector is realistic for an offline product, and asked to rely on another solution if that burden is too high. Hardware feasibility does not answer that concern. The current 161 authored records are unreviewed development material; the earlier family/per-type quotas are pilot coverage checks, not evidence of statistically sufficient training data or an achievable release quality level. Do not imply that reaching those counts makes training worthwhile or the resulting model reliable.

Defer custom MiniLM fine-tuning as the release path. Stop expanding the corpus merely to fill training quotas. Preserve all sources, failed/successful experiments, annotations and pending review artifacts. No optimizer training has occurred. The owner's offer to review examples remains useful for evaluation; it does not commit them to ongoing training-data curation. Existing examples are already observed and must never be called a fresh final holdout.

Prefer a maintained pretrained detector, tested local recognizers and deliberate human review, within an explicit supported scope. Keep one user-visible Vietnamese pack, pinned local artifacts and manual-only updates. Maintenance still includes verifying upstream license/provenance and version changes, testing Vietnamese behavior, offsets and category boundaries, and shipping reviewed pack versions. Outsourcing pretraining does not outsource product validation or permit auto-updating weights.

The prior comparison rejects all six evaluated configurations for the full proposed scope. This decision does not reverse those failures, promote the existing pair or quietly lower numerical quality thresholds. Reassess whether an existing single detector is useful within a narrower contextual role alongside independently qualified local rules; clearly document unsupported categories and measured losses. If this cannot meet the agreed workflow requirements, keep it experimental rather than treating custom training or a third model as the automatic answer.

A manual metadata check of Fastino's GLiNER2 multilingual PII model is not grounds for a swap: its publisher lists seven European training languages, no Vietnamese evaluation, and unreviewed synthetic training data. See the [publisher model card](https://huggingface.co/fastino/gliner2-privacy-filter-PII-multi), checked on this date. “Multilingual” alone is not Vietnamese qualification. No new model was downloaded or executed during this reassessment.

Next work should be a bounded build-versus-adopt comparison based on the existing candidate evidence: exact required capabilities, local-rule versus contextual responsibilities, known misses/excess masking, packaged cost, maintenance burden, and any justified new candidate. Propose scope changes openly before changing product claims or release gates. Obtain human review of useful evaluation examples, not an open-ended commitment to annotate a training corpus. No cloud inference, telemetry or collection of customer corrections is introduced.

The annotation review page under `scripts/review-corpus/` was prepared before this revision; its state logic passes three tests, but browser interaction has not yet been qualified. It is optional development tooling, not a finished app feature or a source of approvals. The pending ledger remains unchanged. Do not describe creating the page as completing human review.
