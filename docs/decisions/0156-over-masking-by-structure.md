# 0156 — Over-masking: headings in capitals, transaction wording, table rows and columns

Date: 26 October 2026. Status: implemented. Owner: the agreed next step after 0154, "over-masking, measured on seeds 1–7 plus the private set, with no new leaks allowed", under the owner's standing instruction to judge every change on whole sets rather than on the file that prompted it (0152).

## Method

After 0154 the synthetic set (0153) leaked nothing but over-masked 81–93 values per seed (seed 1: 83) on seven seeds, 189 documents; the owner's invoices (0152) were clean. A diagnostic run attributed every over-masked value to the detector, type and surrounding text that produced it. All of them were model spans, in four families: headings written in capitals (about half), unaccented wording in bank transaction text, goods in Word tables, and relations and job titles in a CV's tables. Each change below is a structure of the document, not a list of the values that were masked, and was measured on all seven seeds and the private set after every step.

## Changes

1. **Word and ODT tables read by row** (`docx.ts`, `odt.ts`). The Word reader gave each table cell its own paragraph, so a rule that looks at a row (the goods cell of 0155, the rules below) never saw one, and goods were masked in a Word invoice but not in the same invoice as PDF. A table's rows now read as one paragraph each, cells apart by a tab and a cell's paragraphs by a line break, the shape the PDF reader gives rows (0149); a nested table reads inside its cell; an empty or covered cell keeps its column. Each paragraph keeps its own runs, so replacements write back exactly as before, and the paragraph carries its table, so the preview shows Word tables as tables too (a cell may hold several lines; a one-row table has no header styling).
2. **A heading in capitals is not a name** (`model-policy.ts`, `capitalHeading`). A model's name, place or organization in a line or cell that has two words or more and no lowercase letter ("SAO KÊ TÀI KHOẢN", "HỢP ĐỒNG THUÊ NHÀ", "ĐẠI DIỆN BÊN A", "NGƯỜI LAO ĐỘNG (Bên B):", "I. THÔNG TIN BẢN THÂN", "YÊU CẦU TRA SOÁT GIAO DỊCH") needs the evidence of its type: a name starts with a family name and every word is a syllable names are made of; a place has a digit or an address word in the cell (số, đường, phường, xã, quận, …); an organization has its legal form or kind (công ty, TNHH, ngân hàng, trường, bệnh viện, …). A parenthesis and a trailing colon are not part of the test; a cell of one word is left to the score rule of 0081. The shape test of 0154 (`looksLikeName`) was not enough on its own: "QUAN HỆ GIA ĐÌNH", "LÝ LỊCH" and "TRÌNH CÔNG TÁC" all start with a family name and are all syllables, so `person-cues.ts` gains a list of the syllables Vietnamese given and middle names are made of, with their tone marks (about four hundred, broad rather than complete), and `looksLikeKnownName` requires every word to be one. The list is used only where a line in capitals carries no other evidence; names after a label, a form of address or a signature note are found by the rules of 0154 whatever their syllables. The national header ("CỘNG HÒA XÃ HỘI CHỦ NGHĨA VIỆT NAM", "Độc lập - Tự do - Hạnh phúc") joins the boilerplate phrases, and the PDF reader no longer joins a heading in capitals with the centred line below it ("SAO KÊ TÀI KHOẢN" over "Từ ngày …"), which had hidden the heading inside a line with lowercase letters.
3. **Transaction wording is not a name** (`transactionCell`). A transaction row of a statement is a tabbed row with a date cell and an amount cell. In a cell of it written without tone marks ("TRA LUONG THANG 9", "CT DEN", "Thanh toan QR", "Rut tien ATM", "Lai nhap von"), the models, trained on accented Vietnamese, read the wording as names, places and organizations; such a span is dropped unless it has the shape of a name. People there are found by their position (0154) and companies by their legal form in capitals (0154), not by a model.
4. **Column headings** (`wordingColumn`). A tabbed cell in a column whose heading says it holds wording ("Quan hệ", "Chức vụ", "Chức danh", "Nghề nghiệp", "Vị trí", "Trình độ", "Tên hàng hóa…", "Hàng hóa", "Sản phẩm", "Mặt hàng", "Đơn vị tính", "Số lượng", "Đơn giá", "Thành tiền", "Loại") holds no person, place or organization: "Bố" and "Mẹ", "Nhân viên" and "Trưởng nhóm", "Giáo viên" and "Kinh doanh", goods. The heading is the same cell of the first row of the run of rows with as many cells above this one (rows read as one paragraph each sit a blank line apart in the scanned text). This is the start of "use structure before word lists" from 0152; the goods-cell rule of 0155 stays for a row whose header is out of reach.
5. **Smaller findings from the same diagnostic.** A tab never joins two model name pieces ("Bố\tBùi Bảo Trang" had become one name). An amount with thousands separators ("69.600.000", "18.000.000") is not a phone number, however the library parses its digits (`contact.ts`). A currency code alone ("Loại tiền: VND") is neither a place nor an organization. "Giới tính", "Dân tộc", "Ghi nợ", "Ghi có", "Số dư" and "Loại tiền" are field names.

No dependency was added.

## Known limits

- A name in a line in capitals with no label, signature note or form of address, whose given name is outside the syllable list, is no longer masked by the models alone. The list is a resource to extend, not a rule to tune to a document; the private set and the synthetic set measure it.
- A company in a transaction cell without a legal prefix ("TRA LUONG THANG 9 VINGROUP") is not masked: the models' word is not trusted there.
- A merged header cell shifts the column index of the cells after it against its heading, and a heading more than one batch (8,000 characters) above its row is out of reach; the goods-cell rule of 0155 then covers invoices, and nothing covers a relation column.
- In a Word row, a replacement that crosses a cell boundary writes its alias into the first run it covers and empties the rest, as in any paragraph.
